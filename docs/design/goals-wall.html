Goals tab: the wall (W2) in the Lantern theme

Each goal is a note pinned to the wall. The front shows this month: the milestone in your own words and a bar. Tap a note to flip it to this week: the weekly action, 7 day squares (green = done, gray = a gap, never red) and the range from step ④. Your why from step ⑥ (D217) sits on the goal detail, readable any time, and comes back on a bad day. The bar stays Today · + · Goals. «+» still opens a new task. A new goal starts from the dashed note on the wall.
