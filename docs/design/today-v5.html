Today v5: the cast and the night themeThe picked character's theme (D187: knight cyan, vampire red, goth purple, wizard silver gray) colors the whole screen: sky, glow, pixel dialog box, «الان» card, buttons. Done stays green and due stays amber in every theme. Your character sits at the top and says the one thing to do now; the big card under it is that thing, one tap to tick. The date takes the theme color. No week row: Today shows only today. The pixel bar has one block per task for today. Tasks are grouped by part of the day; the current part is cyan. Accent green = done, amber = «موعدش رسیده». Moods follow your day, never your misses: calm at the start, busy after the first tick, happy when all is done; at night the calm look with a dimmer glow (never the sleepy one). Nervous and cross are never shown here. Tap the character for its next line. Use the buttons above to switch character and time of day (in the app, the pick lives in Settings).
