هدهد یا پیر؟Round 2: grown-up

Round 1 was too happy, childish and cartoonish. Round 2 draws them as real-looking illustrations: natural proportions, real colors, soft light from the upper left, calm faces, no sticker border. They also move less: a grown-up character shows feeling through small things (the hoopoe's crest and head, the old man's staff and eyes), never by jumping around. The lines are calmer too.

Accent The hoopoe's branch sprouts in it; the magician's staff glows in it.

Meet them

راه از همین قدم شروع می‌شه.

هدهدThe hoopoe

Who
The guide from Attar's منطق‌الطیر, who leads the birds through seven valleys to the Simorgh. He walks your road with you; you do the work.
Feeling
The crest. Folded most of the time, half raised when he's curious, a full fan for a big moment. His head lifts or lowers with it.
Look
A real Eurasian hoopoe on a branch: cinnamon body, zebra wings, the long curved bill.
Voice
Quiet and sure, a friend who knows the road. یک قدم دیگه.
خوش اومدی. از کجا شروع کنیم؟

پیرThe old magician

Who
An old mage in a grey-blue robe and a worn hat, with a long beard and a staff. The Persian root is Hafez's پیر مغان, the wise elder of the poems.
Feeling
His staff's stone and his eyes. It glows softly every day, blazes when he lifts it for a big moment, and dims at night.
Look
His face stays half in the shadow of the brim. That keeps him mysterious and dignified, not cute.
Voice
Slow, warm, a little dry. این جادو نبود. کار خودت بود.

What changes for the build

Round 1, cartoonRound 2, grown-up
FormatVector, one rig, about 17 poses.Still vector: sharp at any size, small in the APK, tinted from the accent. Each state is drawn on its own, so expect 4–6 states, not 17.
MotionA bouncy pop on each change.A slow cross-fade; the crest or the glow changes, nothing jumps.
Small sizesThe whole figure reads at 64 dp.A head crop for the Today row and the icon.
More real than thisPainted or photographic art would be bitmaps from an illustrator or an image model: a file per state and screen density, a bigger APK, and no accent tint. This level is close to what hand-built vectors do well.
My take

The hoopoe works better in this style. A real hoopoe is already striking, so realism makes him better, and at 24 px his crest and bill still read. The old magician is the stronger presence on a big screen, but at small sizes he's a hat and a beard, and he leans toward Gandalf. If you pick him, I'd make him more Persian: a tall felt hat, a patterned robe hem, and a lamp in place of the staff.
