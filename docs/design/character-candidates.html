هدهد یا پیر؟Hoopoe or Old Magician

The app is becoming a goal app for any goal: training, studying, a language, a habit. Both candidates are drawn in the current «پیکر» sticker style (ink outline, white die-cut border, soft shadow, slight tilt), so the only difference is who they are. They do the same six moments with the same rules: no sad faces, no guilt, praise only for what you did.

Accent The hoopoe wears it as a scarf; the magician as his hat and robe.

Meet them

هدهدThe hoopoe

Who
The guide from Attar's منطق‌الطیر, who leads the birds through seven valleys to the Simorgh. Here he walks your road with you. He doesn't do the work; you do.
Feeling
The crest. Fanned up for a big moment, half up most of the time, folded flat for night and quiet days.
Colors
His own: cinnamon body, black-and-white wings and tail. Only the scarf takes the accent, so he never reads as a green bird like Duo.
Voice
A friend on the road, a little cheeky. کل امروز! تاجم سیخ شد.

پیرThe old magician

Who
A kind old wizard. The Persian root is Hafez's پیر مغان, "the elder of the magi", the wise guide of the poems. He has seen every road and knows there's no shortcut.
Feeling
Face and hands: bushy eyebrows, eyes, a wave, a hand on his beard. The star on his staff sparkles at big moments.
Colors
Hat and robe take the accent; gold trim and stars, white beard.
Voice
Warm, wise, gently funny. His signature line: این جادو نبود. کار خودت بود. The only magic is doing it again.

My take

The hoopoe still wins for me, narrowly. He is the more distinctive mark, he is unmistakably Iranian, and the seven-valleys story gives the whole app a structure, not only a mascot. The magician is the warmer talker, and این جادو نبود. کار خودت بود. is the best single line on this page. If you love the old man, a Persian version of him (a sage's tall felt hat in place of the pointed one) would fix his weakest point.
