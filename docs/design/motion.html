BeAesthetic motion

A working demo of the motion plan: calm by default, generous at the moments that matter. Tap things the way you would in the app. On a phone, the badge at the top of each screen shows where the app would vibrate. Use slow motion to study an animation, and "Remove animations" to see what the app does when the phone asks for no motion.
