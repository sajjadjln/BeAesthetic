Type and color

The approved Today screen in six Persian fonts, then in four accent colors. Everything you can tap works: tiles tick, the part-of-day icons switch, the leaf turns on the minimum day. Use the bar to switch the accent, dark/light, and the font of the color section.

1 · Fonts

Same screen and colors, six fonts. All six are free (SIL Open Font License), so any of them can be bundled in the APK. Each was checked for the Persian letters پ چ ژ گ and Persian digits ۰–۹.

2 · Accent color

The accent is the color of «done» and progress: the ring, ticked tiles, the dashboard squares, buttons. Amber («مهم», «موعدش رسیده») never changes. The special-day color shifts when it would look too close to the accent. Gray gaps stay gray.

3 · Where you pick it

Settings → «ظاهر و زبان» (the «زبان» tile, renamed). Tap a color and the whole app follows, this page included.
