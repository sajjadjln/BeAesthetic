The knight's + button as a sword

Three pixel-art swords for the knight's new-task button, next to the current one. The sword points down, so its crossguard and blade still make a +. Tap each button to see its press animation. A shine runs down the blade every 4 seconds.

Why it still reads as "add": point-down, the guard is the + bar and the blade is the + stem. The sword is the same in RTL and LTR, so nothing flips.
Tap target: options 2 and 3 are narrower than they look, but the button around them is 64 dp wide, above the 48 dp minimum.
Other characters: this is knight-only. The vampire keeps its coffin, and the wizard and the goth keep the pixel square until each gets its own idea.
Build cost: all three are one vector drawable (pixel rects) plus a translate animation in Compose. No bitmap.
