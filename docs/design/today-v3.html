Today, three ways

All three show the tasks for the part of the day the phone's clock is in, with no switcher to tap. All three keep every task one tap away in a section that stays folded, and all three put «کار جدید» on the first screen. They differ in how much the screen shows at once.

The phones work: tick tasks, open «همه‌ی کارها», and tap + to add a task. Change the clock below to see the screen follow the time of day. The sample day is Wednesday 29 Mehr, a training day.

Phone clock

The same in all three

The clock picks the part

The part comes from your reminder times, as the app already does for the reminders: morning until the midday reminder, and so on. Night runs until 04:00, when the day rolls over.

Earlier tasks don't follow you

At 14:30 an unticked morning task isn't shown in «الان». It's still in «همه‌ی کارها» with its part, so you can tick it late. No list of what's left from earlier, no gray warnings.

+ opens the simple sheet

Name, then «کِی؟» on «امروز», and the part is the one the clock is in (D128). «تنظیمات بیشتر» still opens the full form.

Unchanged

The workout card, «هر وقت» (now inside «همه»), minimum day (the leaf), one-off cards and the health and backup rows, which sit at the bottom.
