The goth girl's + button as a cat

Three cats for the goth girl's new-task button, next to the current bitten moon. They're drawn smooth like the moon, not in pixels, and use her purple. Tap each button to see its press. Each cat also moves a little while idle.

Reading as "add": option 1 keeps a big dark + in the middle of the face. In options 2 and 3 the + is smaller, so they rely more on the button's place in the middle of the bar.
Idle motion: one ear twitches, the eyes blink or the tail swishes, every few seconds. All of it stops when the phone's animations are off.
Build cost: each is a few Compose paths, like the moon today. No bitmap.
