پیکر، رفیق باشگاهیThe character

The logo's sticker guy, brought to life as a person who lives in the app, the way Duo lives in Duolingo. His name is «پیکر», like the app. He's a hype gym buddy: warm, a bit goofy, proud of you. He sits small on Today and gets big at the moments that matter. He wears something new each chapter. Unlike Duo, he never guilt-trips: no sad faces at you, no missed days, no "come back" pushes. Pick an accent and a chapter below; every پیکر on the page follows. Tap the big one.

Who
Your gym buddy for the 23 weeks. Not a coach who grades you, not a pet you have to feed. He's on your side, always.

Persian, first person, informal, short (one line, ≤ ~8 words). Hypes what you did: ایول! این بازوها مال خودته. Small jokes, gym slang, never sarcasm.
Praises
What you did: ticks, sets, records, showing up, coming back. Never your body, weight or looks.

Sad, angry or disappointed at you. Never names, counts or hints at a missed day. His lowest mood is sleepy.
Color
Your accent. The launcher icon stays green.
Where
Small on Today, under the ring, always. Big at moments: 100% day, record, comeback, chapter start, check-in, Friday wins, recaps, finish. Plus empty screens and onboarding.

Poses

Seventeen poses from one rig: the same shapes as the logo (ink outline, white sticker border, shadow), so every pose reads as the same guy. Each pose has a face and, for some, a prop. The app picks the pose from the state of the day; you never choose it.

On the screens

Today gets one new row under the ring and week dots: پیکر (about 64 dp) and one line in a bubble. It replaces the old one-line «خوش برگشتی». The line changes when the day changes: morning, a tick, a finished part, 100%, minimum day, night. At the big moments he stands centered and larger. The interactive Today in screens.html has him too.

An outfit for each chapter

Something new each chapter brings novelty over the months, and it can never go backwards: outfits come with the calendar, not with your ticks, so nothing is earned or lost. He shows the new one on the chapter-start card. The outfit stays on in every pose until the next chapter.

What he says

Sample lines, a few per moment. In the app they live in string resources (Persian and English), several per moment, and the same line doesn't come back within 7 days. Numbers in a line are real («۸ تا بارفیکس»), never invented praise.

Rules that keep him kind

No guilt, ever

He never names, counts or hints at a missed day. After a gap he's just there, in the normal pose for the time of day. He is never sad, angry, crying or disappointed at you.

دیروز نیومدی.۳ روزه خبری ازت نیست…ناراحتم کردی.فقط ۲ تا زدی؟یه کم شکم آوردی!
Not in notifications

Reminders stay plain and factual. No "پیکر misses you" pushes, no character in the notification. He lives inside the app only.

Not a pet

No hunger, health, levels, coins or shop. He can't get sick, sad or leave. Outfits come with the calendar. Nothing to maintain, so he can't become a chore.

Motion follows action

No idle loops: he doesn't bounce, breathe or blink while you read. He changes pose when something happens: a quick pop (scale 0.88 → 1, bouncy spring, ~340 ms) and the bubble fades in (200 ms). "Remove animations" makes it instant. No sound.

One tap of play

Tapping him on Today gives a new line and a flex or a wink. It's the only thing he does on his own. It never logs anything.

Can be hidden

«ظاهر و زبان» → «پیکر در صفحه‌ی امروز», on by default. Off: the Today row goes away and the moments fall back to their plain text. If he gets old, he goes, and the app still works.

How he's built

PartPlan
DrawingOne Compose ImageVector per pose, built from the same parts as this board (docs/design/piker.js is the reference rig). Body color = the accent; ink, paper and props are fixed. Outfits are a separate overlay layer. No Lottie, no bitmaps (D76).
Choosing pose and linePure Kotlin in :domain: BuddyPicker(dayState, moment, history) → (pose, lineId). Unit-tested: no line comes back within 7 days, and each moment only gets its own poses. The line pool has no guilt lines to pick from.
LinesString arrays per moment in strings.xml (fa and en). Placeholders for real numbers. The last-shown dates live in the usage log (D59).
MotionAnimatedContent with the bouncy spring for the pose; fade for the bubble; motion tokens from the theme. Light vibration only where the moment already has one.
WhenSlice 2 (Today complete): the Today row, the day poses and the line picker. Each big moment arrives with its own slice (record in slice 4, check-in in 7, recaps in 8b and 10).
