First mockups for the new identity (D148–D162). The look is the app's own (tokens, Estedad, tiles) with no character (D159). Two tabs only: Today and Goals (D160). The Goals tab is a wall with goals pinned to it like notes (D161). The dashboard grid is a screen behind the wall's grid button, and it only appears once a goal exists. Goals, dates and numbers are examples, not your data. Tap the tiles on Today to tick them.
