## 4. Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities for BeAesthetic

| Strengths vs. the field | Weaknesses vs. the field | Opportunities |
|---|---|---|
| Only design found that unites routines + training + proof | No app yet; every week without it is a week without reminders | **Decaying strength score** (Loop) as an internal measure for routines, without showing a streak |

| Offline, no account, verified backups: answers the #1 trust failure (data loss) and survives Iranian shutdowns | Reminder-heavy design risks **dependency** (habits stop when the app stops) | **Ghost-overlay camera** before the 2nd photo set |
| Persian-first, Jalali, Saturday week | No novelty after week ~6 (backlog "5-week phases" is the only planned fix) | **Program chapters** aligned to Jalali months; Yalda as the halfway marker; Nowruz as the finish |
| Reminder health check is unique in the set | Onboarding not designed (fine for 1 user; blocks a release) | For a future release: "hard program, kind rules" against 75 Hard; offline-proof against foreign apps |

## 5. Bottom line

BeAesthetic's design already avoids the biggest mistakes competitors make (punishment, resetting streaks, surprise billing, data loss, cloud dependence). Where competitors are **better** is in (a) **warmth and delight** in the moment of completion (Finch, Duolingo), (b) **novelty over months** (Fabulous journeys, Duolingo events, Strava's Year in Sport), and (c) **photo proof tooling** (ghost overlay, compare). Those are the gaps worth closing; `06` and `08` say how.
